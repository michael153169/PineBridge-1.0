MALAYSIA'S EQUITY MARKET

Understanding Bursa Malaysia

Bursa Malaysia is Malaysia's principal securities exchange. It provides a marketplace for listed securities and is an important part of the country's capital-market ecosystem.

For new market learners, understanding company listings, market segments and price movements can help establish a stronger foundation for financial literacy.
