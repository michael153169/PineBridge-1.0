WHO WE ARE

Focused on Malaysian equities.

PineBridge Investments provides a digital environment for learning about the Malaysian stock market. Our website introduces equity-market concepts while our client platform offers a virtual account experience.

We believe that clear information, thoughtful design and an understanding of risk are important starting points for anyone exploring how equity markets work.

All balances and performance figures in our simulation are virtual. They are not actual investments, real trading records or withdrawable funds.
